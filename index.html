I'm Shervin Shirzad, a freelance graphic designer focused on digital design, visual communication, social media creatives, and brand-focused visuals. I enjoy creating modern visuals that feel clear, engaging, and visually refined.

With strong attention to detail and a minimalist approach, I aim to turn ideas into visuals that connect naturally with people.

Over the years, I've worked across branding, advertising, and digital content design, with a stronger focus on social media visuals and creative digital experiences. My work combines clean aesthetics, thoughtful composition, and modern visual storytelling.
